Title
AccessFinish — The AI says "fixed". We check the user can finish. | NVIDIA Nemotron

Description
Ask an AI to fix a broken form and it will say it's fixed. AccessFinish doesn't trust the confirmation page: it drives the real journey in a browser, reads what was actually saved in the database, reopens it in a fresh browser, and feeds the evidence back to NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Super (Nebius Token Factory) until the saved outcome is right.

Live results (54 runs on GitHub Actions, $6.38 of Nemotron in total):
- Saved phone: fixed and verified in 27 of 27 runs, 26 on the first attempt.
- Keyboard date, a real upstream bug: fixed by Nemotron with no hints in 2 of 10 runs of the final agent versions. Along the way, 77 attempts showed a "booked" confirmation while the booking was saved, or reopened, with the wrong date; AccessFinish rejected every one and recorded which code caused it.
