Title
AccessFinish | AI source repair to verified booking | Nebius x NVIDIA

Description
AccessFinish helps developers and accessibility QA engineers review AI-generated source repairs and verify the intended booking outcome.

NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Super on Nebius Token Factory proposed five source edits for a keyboard booking case in Easy!Appointments. The diagnosis and repair direction were supplied. After exact approval, AccessFinish built a fresh isolated fixture, performed the keyboard task, checked the saved appointment through a separate SELECT-only reader, and reopened it in a fresh browser. Preservation and regression controls also had to pass.

This 140-second demonstration uses actual UI captures with edited timing. It shows a new isolated execution of a recorded model proposal. It makes no new model request and is not a speed benchmark.

All five frozen attempts on this one guided, exposed case are retained: two complete passes, two build failures and one calendar-navigation failure. Independent browser reviews are pending. No general repair accuracy, screen-reader study, measured time saving or adoption is claimed.
