AccessFinish — The AI says "fixed". We check the user can finish. | NVIDIA Nemotron

Description
Ask an AI to fix a broken form and it will say it's fixed. AccessFinish doesn't trust the confirmation page: it drives the real journey in a browser, reads what was actually saved in the database, reopens it in a fresh browser, and feeds the evidence back to NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Super (Nebius Token Factory) until the saved outcome is right.

Live results (14 runs on GitHub Actions, $0.41 of Nemotron in total):
- Saved phone: fixed and verified on the first attempt in 7 of 7 runs.
- Keyboard date: not fixed yet. 15 attempts showed a "booked" confirmation while the wrong day was saved; AccessFinish rejected every one and recorded why.
